Dear Satyam,
12.08.2026

2019 mein PUBG ke through tumse mila tha. Sach kahun to tab bilkul idea nahi tha ki ek random game se shuru hui dosti itne saal tak chalegi.

Itne saalon mein games bhi badle, time bhi badla aur life bhi kaafi change hui. Lekin kuch friendships ko daily conversations ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Tum unhi friends mein se ho jinke saath connection bana rehta hai, chahe kitna bhi time beet jaaye.

Aaj tumhara birthday hai, aur bas itna kehna hai — I hope tumhari life mein hamesha happiness, success, peace aur bahut saari achhi memories rahein.

Keep smiling.

Keep winning.

And keep being the same person.

Happy Birthday, Satyam. ❤️
